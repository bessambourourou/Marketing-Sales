# Marketing Analytics Portfolio Project

A data presentation analyzing a year of marketing performance across three areas: **conversion rates**, **customer engagement**, and **customer feedback**. It identifies what is underperforming and recommends actions to improve each area.

## Key Findings

| Area | Headline | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| Conversion | Strong December rebound after a weak October | 10.2% in Dec vs. 5.0% in Oct |
| Engagement | Views decline through the year; engaged users still interact | 15.37% click-through rate |
| Feedback | Ratings are stable but below target | ~3.7 average vs. 4.0 target |

## Analysis

### 1. Conversion Rates

- **General trend:** Conversion varied through the year, with more products converting successfully in months like February and July. This points to seasonal peaks and room to improve in weaker months through targeted interventions.
- **Lowest month:** May had the lowest overall conversion rate (**4.3%**), with no product standing out. Marketing strategy and promotions for this period need revisiting.
- **Highest month:** January had the highest overall conversion rate (**18.5%**), driven largely by Ski Boots (**150%** conversion), likely due to seasonal demand and effective marketing.

### 2. Customer Engagement

- **Declining views:** Views peaked in February and July, then declined from August onward, indicating reduced audience engagement in the second half of the year.
- **Low interaction rates:** Clicks and likes stay consistently low relative to views, suggesting a need for more engaging content or stronger calls to action.
- **Content type performance:** Blog content drove the most views (especially April and July). Social media and video content held steady at slightly lower engagement.

### 3. Customer Feedback

- **Ratings distribution:** Reviews skew positive: 140 at 4 stars and 135 at 5 stars, versus 26 at 1 star and 57 at 2 stars.
- **Sentiment:** 275 reviews are positive and 82 are negative, with a smaller number of mixed and neutral.
- **Opportunity:** Mixed-sentiment reviews are a chance to turn partly satisfied customers into clearly satisfied ones. Products rated below 3.5 need focused attention.

## Goals

| Goal | Insight sought |
|---|---|
| **Increase conversion rates** — identify factors affecting conversion and recommend improvements | Key funnel stages where visitors drop off, and how to optimize them |
| **Enhance customer engagement** — determine which content types drive the most engagement | Interaction levels by content type, to inform content strategy |
| **Improve customer feedback scores** — understand common themes in reviews | Recurring positive and negative feedback, to guide product and service improvements |

## Recommended Actions

1. **Increase conversion rates — target high-performing categories.** Focus marketing on products with proven conversion (Kayaks, Ski Boots, Baseball Gloves). Run seasonal promotions or personalized campaigns in peak months (e.g., January and September).
2. **Enhance engagement — revitalize content strategy.** Experiment with interactive video and user-generated content, and optimize call-to-action placement in social and blog content, especially in historically low-engagement months (September–December).
3. **Improve feedback scores — close the loop on mixed and negative reviews.** Analyze them for common issues, build improvement plans, and follow up with dissatisfied customers to resolve problems and encourage re-rating, moving the average toward the 4.0 target.
